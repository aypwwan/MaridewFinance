App Lock

Lock the app automatically after a period of inactivity. Unlocking requires your account password — handy on shared machines.

The lock screen verifies your password without ending your session; you can also sign out from there. Mouse, keyboard and scroll activity counts as “active”.

Custom Categories

Create your own categories for transactions and budgets. Rename or delete them anytime — they appear everywhere the built-in ones do.

Built-in categories (Housing, Food & Dining, Salary, …) are always available and cannot be removed. Deleting a custom category keeps its past transactions — they just keep the old name.

Used in transactions: 0 · Transaction categories live independently of budget names (create a budget for any category on the Budgets tab).

Tip: type an unknown name on the Budgets tab and it becomes a new category too.
